# Cartesian

**Vibe:** Quiet warm-neutral palette with classical Playfair serifs — unhurried, considered

**Layout:** Two-tone warm-neutral grounds with italic Playfair Display headlines. Wide gutters, generous leading, thin neutral rules. Uppercase Inter labels in soft accent.

**Typography:**
- Display: `Playfair Display` (400/700)
- Body: `Inter` (400/500)

**Colors:**
```css
:root {
  --ca-bg: #ede8e0;
  --ca-bg-2: #e2dbd1;
  --ca-text: #1a1a1a;
  --ca-text-2: #5a5a5a;
  --ca-accent: #8a8178;
  --ca-line: #b8b0a4;
}
```

**Signature Elements:**
- Italic Playfair Display with bold roman emphasis (em swap)
- Two warm-neutral grounds rotating per chapter
- Wide gutters, generous leading throughout
- Thin neutral rules under section markers
- Lowercase chapter roman numerals in Inter spaced caps
