# Editorial Forest

**Vibe:** Forest green + dusty pink + warm cream — quiet, intentional quarterly-review aesthetic

**Layout:** Cream paper canvas with italic Source Serif 4 headlines in forest green. Dusty pink circle/dot accents. Optional green-flooded slide for editorial quotes. JetBrains Mono labels.

**Typography:**
- Display: `Source Serif 4` (400 italic)
- Body: `Source Serif 4` (400/500)
- Mono / accent: `JetBrains Mono (labels)`

**Colors:**
```css
:root {
  --ef-green: #2e4a2a;
  --ef-green-deep: #243a21;
  --ef-pink: #e89cb1;
  --ef-cream: #efe7d4;
  --ef-ink: #1a1a17;
}
```

**Signature Elements:**
- Italic Source Serif 4 headlines in forest green
- Dusty pink circle accent in corner
- Optional green-flooded slide for editorial quotes
- Warm cream paper ground
- JetBrains Mono uppercase meta labels
- Thin green rules under section markers
