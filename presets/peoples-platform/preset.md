# People's Platform

**Vibe:** Activist poster energy — blue, orange, red on cream, Alfa Slab + Caveat Brush

**Layout:** Cream paper canvas alternating with electric-blue panels. Heavy Alfa Slab One uppercase headlines in red and orange. Caveat Brush handwritten asides. Pill-shape badges in primary colors.

**Typography:**
- Display: `Alfa Slab One` (400)
- Body: `Archivo Narrow` (400/700)
- Mono / accent: `Caveat Brush (handwriting) · DM Mono (labels)`

**Signature Elements:**
